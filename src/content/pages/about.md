# About Shagun

At Shagun, we bring 30 years of rich mehendi tradition to brides and celebrations. Founded by our lead artist, who began practising at her native place before moving to Kota, our team has served brides and families since 2006.

Every design is drawn freehand, using natural mehendi. No black henna, no PPD. For clients who want a faster-stain option, we offer a natural instant formula, and traditional alta is available on request.

Based in Kota, today, we serve brides and families across Kota for bridal, festival, group and home-service bookings — and are available for outstation weddings on request.
